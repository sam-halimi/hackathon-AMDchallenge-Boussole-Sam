Study 1: post-traumatic stress is decided in the first three months

Meta-analysis · 22 samples · 2,106 survivors

75%

of survivors have post-traumatic stress one month after the assault. Still 41% a year later.

In Boussole

Support from the first days, at their own pace: one step at a time, nothing mandatory.

Dworkin, Jaffe, Bedard-Gilligan and Fitzpatrick, Trauma, Violence & Abuse, 24(2), 2023 (online 2021). doi:10.1177/15248380211032213

A meta-analysis pools every serious study on a question: the highest level of evidence. The authors conclude that the first three months are a critical period for natural recovery. That is exactly where Boussole acts.
