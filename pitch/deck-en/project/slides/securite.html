Designed for their safety

“Exit” on every screen

Locks the space and shows a weather page. Always visible, even while scrolling.

No one else reads the story

Encrypted on the phone with their password (AES-256). No server receives it.

No tracking

No cookies, no analytics. Nothing is sent without double consent.

Everywhere, for everyone

Offline, in French and English, readable (AA contrast, 48 px tap targets), no stressful animation.

Boussole · Features · the exit button follows the findings of the University of Cambridge audit (2023)

The Exit button is the most striking feature for the jury: show it live if possible. A person living with their abuser, or on a shared phone, must be able to make the app disappear in a second. Stated limit, also shown in the app: the browser history keeps a trace of the visit.
