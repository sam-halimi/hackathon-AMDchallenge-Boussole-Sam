One app, three uses

For the person

Know what to do, at their own pace

Immediate help, the steps of care, where to go in Montréal, an encrypted story on their phone.

Free, always.

For clinicians

Lose nothing on the first night

Consent step by step, samples sorted by deadline, a timeline of notes, a tamper-proof log.

Licence per centre.

For their rights

A file ready, if they decide

Chosen sections, double consent, sent to a legal service, a callback from a specialized lawyer.

With existing services.

Boussole · Features

Three uses, one promise: the person stays in control. They never pay. The institutions that receive survivors pay for the clinicians’ tool. The legal module doesn’t replace a lawyer: it prepares the file and refers to Rebâtir, the CAVAC, the IVAC.
