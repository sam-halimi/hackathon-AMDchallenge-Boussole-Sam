For clinicians: the first night, nothing lost

Deadlines, without AI. A rules engine sorts samples by the time elapsed.

AI sorts, humans validate. Each line quotes its source; no judgment of the person.

Evidence that holds. A SHA-256 chained log: any edit shows.

Consent step by step, and revocable. The person’s name is never shown to the AI. Nothing goes to the police without their consent.

Boussole · Features · clinicians’ tool (French interface for now), fictional case “Léa”

The clinician saves time on paperwork and misses no deadline. The AI produces no facts: each event quotes its source sentence or is rejected, and the nurse validates line by line. Deadlines come from written, versioned rules, to be validated by each institution’s medical committee. The clinicians’ tool is in French for now; the patient app is fully bilingual.
