Be where people look, before they need it

Campus

CEGEPs and universities

Posters in washrooms and residences, a QR code on the one-stop service page, a slide in the mandatory annual training.

Health

Centres, ERs, pharmacies

A pocket card handed out with emergency contraception or when leaving the designated centre.

Nightlife

Bars and venues

Coasters and posters in the washrooms of partner bars, where drinks get spiked.

Street

Bus shelters and metro

A campaign at the September back-to-school, the highest-risk period on campus.

Online

Where people type “what to do”

Search ranking on “sexual assault what to do”, links from CALACS, Info-aide and one-stop service sites.

Relays

Press and student groups

Pilot launch announced with partner organizations, student media, student associations.

Discretion rule: no shocking images, never the word “victim”. Posters go where people are alone, washrooms and stalls, so the QR code can be scanned unseen.

Boussole · Outreach · mandatory annual training: Bill P-22.1 (2017)

Six channels, all concrete. The most important is campus, because the 2017 law already requires every institution to train its students each year. One slide in that training reaches the whole student body, with no advertising budget. Pharmacies: often the first place a person goes the next day.
