1. Evidence and care have an expiry date

Each bar is how long an action stays possible after the assault. No one knows them on the night itself.

Sources: WHO (2024); Canadian guideline, CMAJ (2025); Fonneløp et al. (2019), 765 cases; ASB 121 (2021); Quebec socio-sanitary protocol (2011) and SPVM (2026)

Read top to bottom: drugs disappear first, then HIV prevention and DNA at 72 hours. After a week, the kit is no longer offered. The kit deadline in Quebec differs by source (5 days in the 2011 protocol, 7 days according to the SPVM in 2026): Boussole shows every deadline with its source.
