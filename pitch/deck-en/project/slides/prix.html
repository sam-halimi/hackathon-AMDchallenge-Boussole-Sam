Price assumptions, to be confirmed during the pilot. Sources: Quebec Treasury Board, thresholds 2026-2027 · STACS DNA, Iowa tender response (2019) · CLPCCD (2019)

The price follows case volume. For higher education, the price is about 8 to 12% of the envelope already received to fight sexual violence (about $79,000 per CEGEP, $181,000 per university on average). The 6-centre, 18-month pilot costs about $111,000, under the $139,000 threshold: direct purchase is possible. Splitting a need to stay under the threshold is forbidden: the pilot is a real, time-limited need.
