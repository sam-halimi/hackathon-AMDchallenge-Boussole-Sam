Study 2: seeking help can become a second wound

Literature review · American Psychologist

“second rape”

How many survivors experience police, hospitals and the courts: seeking help adds to the trauma.

In Boussole

One story, written once, shared only if they decide. No judgment, “I don’t know” always allowed.

Campbell, R., The psychological impact of rape victims’ experiences with the legal, medical, and mental health systems, American Psychologist, 63(8), 2008. doi:10.1037/0003-066X.63.8.702

Rebecca Campbell is the leading reference on “secondary victimization”. Findings of the review: most reported rapes are not prosecuted, and many survivors do not receive comprehensive care in the emergency room. Her 2006 study also shows that survivors supported by an advocate receive more care and experience less distress.
