Complete chain of custody: 92% of kits when the procedure is guided and trained, versus 81%.

Sources: Hendrich et al., Permanente J 2008; Poissant et al., JAMIA 2005; Ciancone et al., Ann Emerg Med 2000; Cottler-Casanova et al., JMIR Med Inform 2025; Sievers et al., J Emerg Nurs 2003

Be honest: the time saved per exam is modest, about 15 minutes, and it is an extrapolation. The real value for a centre is elsewhere: complete records and an unbroken chain of custody. In Geneva, moving from paper to a structured record cut missing information from 64% to 8.5%. Poorly preserved evidence is a complaint that collapses.
