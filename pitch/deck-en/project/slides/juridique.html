The legal side: from token sanctions to justice

Already in Boussole

  • A file where they choose every section
  • Double consent before anything is sent
  • Sent to a legal service (simulated in the beta)
  • A callback from a lawyer trained in sexual violence
  • Their rights explained: complaint, Rebâtir, IVAC

What we are building

  • The three paths, side by side: criminal complaint, civil suit, complaint to the institution
  • Digital evidence kept and timestamped on the phone
  • The IVAC claim prepared, with no deadline for sexual violence
  • Follow-up at the specialized court, with the CAVAC

Boussole doesn’t replace a lawyer: it prepares the file and refers to existing services (Rebâtir, Juripop, Info Justice, CAVAC).

Boussole · Features · legal module to be co-designed with legal services

The link to the Cornell case: a sanction decided by an institution is not a sentence, and a person who doesn’t know the other paths stops there. Boussole shows all three paths at once, with what each can and cannot achieve, and helps keep evidence from day one. IVAC is Quebec’s crime victim compensation program; Rebâtir offers free legal advice to survivors; CAVACs are crime victim assistance centres.
