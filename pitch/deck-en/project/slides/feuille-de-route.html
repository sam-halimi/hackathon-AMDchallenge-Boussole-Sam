Boussole · Business model · base scenario; Ontario: Ontario Network of Sexual Assault/Domestic Violence Treatment Centres (37 centres)

No province without a field partner: resources, steps and deadlines change from province to province and must be validated locally, as in Montréal. The pilot measures repeated retellings, samples taken on time, complete files and clinicians’ administrative time, with a research partner so the results can be published.
